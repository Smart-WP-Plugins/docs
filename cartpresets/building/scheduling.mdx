<Info>
  Scheduling is a Pro feature. On the free version a preset goes live the moment you save it with the status switch on, and never ends on its own. The date controls are visible but locked — and a date already stored still holds a preset back or ends it.
</Info>

A schedule is two dates and a decision about what a customer meets once the second one has passed.

<Media>The Preset details card with the Date field, and the Limits and schedule card with the Ends field</Media>

## The start date

Choose a **Date** in the future, in the **Preset details** card, and the preset reads as **Scheduled** until that day arrives. Following its link before then meets the _This preset is not available yet._ notice.

A preset with no date, or one whose date has passed, is live as soon as its status switch is on.

Dates are whole days, on your store's clock. A start date opens at the first moment of its day; an end date closes at the last, so the preset stays live all through its end day.

<Tip>
  This is what lets a campaign link be built and approved weeks early, then open on its own on launch day without anyone being awake for it.
</Tip>

## The end date

Set **Ends**, in the **Limits and schedule** card, and the preset stops selling once that day is over. It reads as **Expired** afterwards.

A seasonal offer closes itself instead of relying on someone remembering.

A cart filled before the deadline cannot check out after it: checkout asks the same question the link does.

## What happens when it ends

An ended preset is not a broken link. What a customer meets is your choice, set store-wide under **Settings → Links and fallbacks** and overridable per preset. The same choice covers a preset whose purchase limit is used up.

| Level | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Store | **Show a notice that it is unavailable** — on by default. **Redirect to the fallback link** — off by default, which sends the customer to the cart |
| Preset | **Use the Settings default**, or its own pair: the notice switch, and **Redirect to a link** with an address of its own |

The notice and the redirect are separate questions. The redirect decides where the customer lands; the notice decides whether they are told why. The notice defaults to **on**, and that default is deliberate: a customer who follows a link they were given, arrives at an empty cart, and is told nothing has been told your store is broken.

Turn it off once you have somewhere better to send them — typically alongside a redirect to a landing page that explains the offer has closed and shows what else you sell. A preset redirect left without an address falls through to the store's fallback link, and then to your shop page.

<Media>The Limits and schedule card showing the end date and the end-of-life choices</Media>

## Putting an ended preset back on sale

Clearing the end date brings it back. Two ways in:

- The **Ends** field in the editor — clear it and save.
- **Clear end date** in the listing's row menu, which only appears on presets that have one.

<Note>
  Clearing an end date is gated exactly like setting one. On the free version a preset that ended stays ended — moving to the free version must never put a link back on sale that your own schedule had stopped.
</Note>

## Status is derived, never stored

The status badge is computed from the status switch, the dates and the purchase limit every time it is read. Nothing runs on a cron to flip it, and nothing can go stale.

| Badge | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Switch is on, the start date has passed, the end date has not, and the limit is not used up |
| Scheduled | Switch is on, and the start date is in the future |
| Expired | Switch is on, and the end date has passed |
| Sold out | Switch is on, and the purchase limit is used up |
| Inactive | Switch is off — whatever the dates and limits say |

The switch outranks everything, and an expired date outranks a full limit, because a limit can be raised and a date cannot be argued with.

That is why a preset can go live on a site nobody visited overnight: there is nothing to run.

## Settings reference

| Setting | Level | Key | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Date (publish) | Preset | `created_at` | Now |
| Ends | Preset | `end_at` | Empty |
| When a preset reaches its limit or end date | Store | `unavailable_action` | `notice` |
| When a preset reaches its limit or end date | Preset | `unavailable_action` | `global` |
| Redirect address | Preset | `unavailable_url` | Empty |
| Show a notice that it is unavailable | Store | `unavailable_notice` | On |
| Show a notice that it is unavailable | Preset | `unavailable_notice` | On |
