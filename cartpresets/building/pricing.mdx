A discount belongs to an item. Each item resolves a source price and applies its own discount, producing a unit price. The preset's total is the sum of its lines, and nothing is taken off after that.

## Where the base price comes from

```text
base = custom item      → its configured price
       use_regular on   → the product's regular price
       otherwise        → the sale price if there is one, else regular
```

**Use the regular price instead of the sale price** exists because a sale and a preset discount otherwise stack into a margin nobody agreed to. A merchant running 20% off a product and putting it in a preset at 20% off is usually expecting the second 20%, not both.

The control hides itself on a custom item, where it would mean nothing.

## Per-item discounts

| Type | Formula | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | `base` | |
| Percentage | `base × (1 − value/100)` | |
| Fixed amount | `base − value` | **Per unit**, not spread across the quantity |
| Custom price | `value` | A literal override; ignores the base entirely |

Everything is clamped to zero. A 50 fixed discount on a 45 product gives 0, never −5.

<Tip>
  Fixed being per-unit is deliberate. It is consistent with percentage, which is inherently per-unit whatever the quantity is. Two of an item at 5 off is 10 off, not 5.
</Tip>

A discount type chosen with no amount is flagged by the health panel, as a warning rather than a failure.

## The summary agrees with the cart

The editor's pricing summary is computed in your browser, so it can update as you type. The cart's price is computed in PHP.

Those are two implementations of the same arithmetic, and two implementations drift. They are pinned to each other by a shared fixture table that both run against in the test suite, so a formula changed on one side and not the other fails a test rather than silently charging the wrong amount.

If the editor and the cart ever disagree, that is a bug worth reporting.

## Prices are never cached

Item definitions are cached. Prices never are.

A cached price goes stale the moment you edit a product, run a sale, or change a tax rate — and a stale price in a cart is a wrong charge. Every figure is computed from live product prices at the moment it is needed, including the Price column in the listing.

## Coupons on top

Store coupons are refused on preset items by default, per item. Turn on **Allow coupon discounts on this item** where you want them to stack. See [Items](/cartpresets/building/items#coupons).

A preset can also apply a coupon of its own, automatically, the moment the link is followed — see [Automatic coupons](/cartpresets/building/auto-coupon)<Pro />.

## Subscriptions

A preset discount on a subscription product raises a question a one-off purchase does not: does the discount buy the first payment, or every renewal for the life of the subscription?

The free version always reads it as **the first payment**, with the sign-up fee charged. Pro lets you choose per item. See [Subscriptions](/cartpresets/building/subscriptions).
