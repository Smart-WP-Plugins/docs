## Requirements

| | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| WordPress | 6.5 |
| WooCommerce | 8.0 |
| PHP | 7.4 |

Tested up to WordPress 7.1 and WooCommerce 11.0. It supports High-Performance Order Storage and works with both the classic and block-based cart and checkout.

WooCommerce Subscriptions is optional. With it, presets can contain subscription products, and a preset's discount applies to every payment. Pro can also limit the discount to the first few payments and waive the sign-up fee. See [Subscriptions](/cartpresets/building/subscriptions).

## Installing the free version

Search for **CartPresets for WooCommerce** under **Plugins → Add New Plugin**, install, and activate. Or download the zip and upload it under **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**.

## Installing Pro<Pro />

Pro ships as a separate zip from your purchase email or account area. It installs as **CartPresets for WooCommerce Pro**, in the folder `cartpresets-for-woocommerce-pro`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deactivate and delete the free version, if you have it">
    Your presets and settings live in the database, not in the plugin folder, so deleting the free version doesn't touch them. Before you delete, check **Settings → Data management → Delete on uninstall** has nothing ticked. Those options are off unless you turned them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the Pro zip">
    **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**, choose the zip, install, activate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate your licence">
    **Cart Presets → Settings**. The **Licence** card is at the top: paste the key from your purchase email and click **Activate**. If the key isn't linked to an account yet, it asks first and shows the name and email it will send. See [Licence and updates](/cartpresets/licence).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Run one version at a time. If one is already active, activating the other is refused with a message naming both. If both do end up active, the second to load stands down and says so, rather than letting two copies fight over the same tables.
</Warning>

## What activation does

Activation creates four database tables, each with your site's table prefix:

| Table | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `cartpresets_presets` | One row per preset — name, slug, status, dates, caps, settings |
| `cartpresets_items` | The items inside each preset |
| `cartpresets_redemptions` | One row per claim, for purchase limits |
| `cartpresets_slug_history` | Old slugs, so renamed links keep working for 30 days |

It also registers the `/preset/` rewrite rule and flushes permalinks once.

On a first activation it stores the shipped default for every setting. A store that already has settings keeps them untouched, whether that's a reinstall or a switch between versions. The carrier product isn't created at activation. When you need one<Pro />, **Settings → Carrier product** offers **Create carrier product** or lets you choose a product you already have.

## Moving between versions

Both versions read and write the same tables, so switching either way migrates nothing and loses nothing.

**Free to Pro.** Your existing presets are untouched. Any Pro setting you couldn't reach before is still at its default, ready to set.

**Pro to free.** Your presets, items and settings stay exactly as they are. The Pro controls are locked and tagged **Pro**, and your stored answers stay on the record. Reinstalling Pro turns them back on rather than asking you to set them up again.

Two things stop *applying* rather than staying on, because leaving them running would be worse:

- **An automatic coupon** is no longer applied. A discount you can't see or switch off would go on discounting orders.
- **Custom items** aren't added to the cart. They stay with the preset and come back if you upgrade. The editor marks them **Not added on the free version**, and the listing and editor totals leave them out.

A schedule already set and a cap already filled are still honoured. Those switch a link **off**, and moving to the free version must not put a link back on sale that your own schedule or cap had stopped.

## Uninstalling

Deleting the plugin removes nothing by default: not your presets, not your settings, not the carrier product. Each category has its own opt-in switch under **Settings → Data management**. See [Data and uninstall](/cartpresets/settings/data-and-uninstall).
