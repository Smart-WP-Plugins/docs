This walks through one preset end to end: three products, a discount and a link you can paste into a newsletter. It all works on the free version.

## 1. Create the preset

Go to **Cart Presets → Add New Preset**.

Give it a name. The slug is the part that appears in the link, so "Starter Kit" becomes `starter-kit`. Leave the slug empty and it's built from the name when you save. Type your own if you want something shorter. If a slug is already taken a number is added, so a save is never refused over it.

The internal description is for your team. Customers never see it.

<Media>The Preset details card with a name, slug and description filled in</Media>

## 2. Add items

In the **Items** card, click **Add store product** and choose a product. Repeat for each one you want in the set.

For a variable product, the picker asks you to choose the variation. You have to answer every attribute. A preset has nobody to ask at cart time, so "Any" isn't an option here.

Expand a row to set:

- **Quantity** — how many of this item the link adds.
- **A discount** — a percentage, a fixed amount off each unit, or a custom price that replaces the product's own.
- **Quantity rules** — whether the customer can change the amount in the cart, and within what range.

<Media>The Items card with three products, one row expanded showing the discount and quantity controls</Media>

## 3. Set the price

Each item carries its own discount, and the preset's total is the sum of its lines. There's no second discount on top.

| Discount | What you type | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | — | The product's own price |
| Percentage | `15` | 15% comes off this item |
| Fixed amount | `5` | 5 comes off each unit |
| Custom price | `20` | 20 replaces the product's price |

**Custom price** is the one to use for a kit you've advertised at a set price: give each item the price you want it to carry.

The **Pricing summary** on the right recalculates as you type, and shows the same numbers the customer's cart will. If they disagree, that's a bug: the two are pinned to each other by a shared test fixture.

<Media>The pricing summary sidebar showing regular value, item discounts, average per unit and the preset total</Media>

## 4. Check the health panel

In the sidebar, **Health checks** runs through a list of questions about the preset and answers each one. Before the first save the card is titled **Before your link exists**.

A **warning** is something that will work but may not be what you meant. A **failure** blocks the save: a variation with an unanswered attribute, a discount switched on with no amount, or no items at all. Click any check to jump to the control it's about.

<Media>The health checks panel with one warning and one failure, the failure expanded</Media>

## 5. Save and share

Click **Save preset**. The **Preset link** card fills in with the live URL:

```text
https://yourstore.com/preset/starter-kit
```

Copy it with the button beside it. The link is live now: anyone who follows it lands on your cart page holding the three items at the price you set.

<Note>
  A preset goes live the moment you save it with the status switch on. There's no separate publish step. To build it now and sell it later, use a [schedule](/cartpresets/building/scheduling)<Pro />.
</Note>

## 6. Try it yourself

Open the link in a private window, so you arrive as a customer would.

You should land on the cart with the three items in it, each carrying a **Part of preset** label, and a total that matches what the editor quoted.
