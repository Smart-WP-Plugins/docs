This walks through one preset end to end: three products, a discount and a link you can paste into a newsletter. It all works on the free version.

## 1. Create the preset

Go to **Cart Presets → Add New Preset**, or click **New preset** on the listing.

The **Health checks** card on the right starts with two things to do: **Name the preset** and **Add at least one item**. **Save preset** stays unavailable until both are done.

Type a **Name** in the **Preset details** card. The **Slug** fills in as you type: it's the part that appears in the link, so "Starter Kit" becomes `starter-kit`. Type your own if you want something shorter. The button beside it regenerates the slug from the name. If a slug is already taken a number is added, so a save is never refused over it.

The **Internal description** is for your team. Customers never see it.

<Media>The Preset details card with a name, slug and description filled in</Media>

## 2. Add items

In the **Items** card, click **Add store product**. The chooser lists some of your products straight away; type to search by name or SKU. Choose one and click **Use product**. Repeat for each one you want in the set.

The new item opens so you can set it up. The chevron at the end of an item's row opens and closes it. Inside:

- **Quantity**: how many of this item the link adds.
- **Discount type**: **None**, **Percentage**, **Fixed amount off** or **Custom price**, with the amount beside it.
- **In the cart**: whether coupons can discount this line, whether the customer can change the quantity (and within what **Minimum** and **Maximum**), and whether they can remove the item.

For a variable product, choose each option in the item, such as **Choose a color**. Every option needs an answer. A preset has nobody to ask at cart time, so "Any" isn't possible here, and the preset can't be saved until every option is set.

The up and down arrows at the start of each row set the order items land in the cart.

<Media>The Items card with three products, one row expanded showing the discount and quantity controls</Media>

## 3. Set the price

Each item carries its own discount, and the preset's total is the sum of its lines. There's no second discount on top.

| Discount | What you type | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | — | The product's own price |
| Percentage | `15` | 15% comes off this item |
| Fixed amount off | `5` | 5 comes off each unit |
| Custom price | `20` | 20 replaces the product's price |

**Custom price** is the one to use for a kit you've advertised at a set price: give each item the price you want it to carry.

The **Pricing summary** on the right recalculates as you type, and shows the same numbers the customer's cart will. Both are checked against the same test data, so if they ever differ, that's a bug.

<Media>The pricing summary sidebar showing regular value, item discounts, average per unit and the preset total</Media>

## 4. Check the health panel

**Health checks**, on the right, runs through a list of questions about the preset. Once the two starting to-dos are done, it shows anything that still needs a look, and a count of the checks that passed.

A **warning** is something that will work but may not be what you meant, like a discount type chosen with no amount. A check marked **Blocks saving** has to be fixed first: a variable product with an option not chosen, say, or no items at all. Click any check to jump to the field it's about.

<Media>The health checks panel with one warning and one failure, the failure expanded</Media>

## 5. Save and share

Click **Save preset** in the bar at the bottom. The **Preset link** card fills in with the live URL:

```text
https://yourstore.com/preset/starter-kit
```

Click **Copy** to copy it. Anyone who follows the link lands on your cart page holding the three items at the price you set.

<Note>
  A new preset goes live the moment you save it. There's no separate publish step. After the first save, a **Link enabled** switch appears at the top of the editor; turn it off to take the link down. It acts straight away, without **Save preset**. To build a preset now and sell it later, give it a **Starts on** date<Pro />. See [Scheduling](/cartpresets/building/scheduling).
</Note>

## 6. Try it yourself

Open the link in a private window, so you arrive as a customer would.

You should land on the cart with the three items in it, each carrying a **Part of preset** label, and a total that matches what the editor quoted.
