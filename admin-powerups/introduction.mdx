WooCommerce runs the store well, but its admin leaves gaps you hit every week. You need an order but only have the product SKU or the total, and the order search can't find it. A customer says the order email never arrived, and there's no record of what was sent. A guest wants their past orders in an account, and WooCommerce has no way to add them. Cash on delivery shows up on a $2,000 order. Each of these usually means installing another plugin.

[Admin Powerups for WooCommerce](https://smartwpplugins.com/plugins/woocommerce/admin-powerups/) fills those gaps in one plugin, with one settings screen under **WooCommerce → Powerups**. Each tool is called a power-up. Only the power-ups you switch on run, so the ones you don't use can't slow your store.

Some power-ups have settings, some add a tool tab, and some just work once they're on.

## The power-ups

### Orders

| Power-up | What it does | Shows in |
|---|---|---|
| [Advanced order search](/admin-powerups/advanced-order-search) | Search orders by customer, phone, SKU, total, coupon, notes and more | Orders list |
| [Autocomplete orders](/admin-powerups/autocomplete-orders) | Mark orders completed automatically when your rules match | Order status after payment |
| [Save order address to customer](/admin-powerups/save-order-address) | Copy an order's address to the customer's profile | Order page |
| [Payment link helper](/admin-powerups/payment-link) | Copy, open or email the payment link for unpaid orders | Order page, Customer pages box |
| [Thank-you page preview](/admin-powerups/thankyou-preview) | Open an order's thank-you page without firing tracking scripts | Order page, Customer pages box |
| [Analytics order filters](/admin-powerups/analytics-filters) | Filter Analytics → Orders by location, payment method, shipping method and customer role | Analytics → Orders |

## How it works

- On a test store with 100,000 orders and 50,000 customers, the Orders and Users lists loaded within 20 milliseconds of their speed without the plugin. Settings that can slow a large store are marked. See [Performance](/admin-powerups/performance).
- High-performance order storage (HPOS), the block and classic checkouts, and multisite are all supported.
- Settings work while a power-up is off, so you can set one up first and switch it on when you're ready.
- The plugin has no tracking, and your data stays on your site.
- Each power-up has a Data section that lists what it saves and lets you delete it. See [Data and uninstall](/admin-powerups/data-and-uninstall).
