Switch to customer lets you see the store exactly as a customer does, then switch back to your own account without logging in again.

**Shows in:** Users list, order page

## Turn it on

Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups** and switch on **Switch to customer**.

<Note>
If the User Switching plugin is active, this power-up stays off. Use one or the other.
</Note>

## Switch to a customer

You'll find a switch link in three places:

- **Users list**: **Switch to** under the user's name.
- **User profile**: **Switch to this customer**.
- **Order page**: **Switch to customer** opens that order in the customer's My Account.

After switching, you land on the page picked in **After switching, open**. From an order, you land on that order in My Account. Links that point off this site are ignored, and you land on the settings page instead.

## Switch back

While switched, a banner at the bottom right says who you're viewing as. Switch back from:

- the banner (**Switch back to …**),
- the admin bar,
- the My Account menu.

## Who you can switch to

- Only users with a role you tick under **Who you can switch to**.
- Never admins, and never anyone with more access than you.
- You need permission to manage WooCommerce and to edit that user.

<Note>
On multisite, only super admins can switch, because WordPress only lets them edit other accounts. Shop managers and site admins don't get the links.
</Note>

If another staff member is already switched into that customer, you're warned and asked to confirm (**Switch anyway**).

## How long a switch lasts

- Your cart and the customer's cart are kept separate.
- A switch ends when you log out or log in again.
- It expires after 12 hours.
- Changing your password ends it.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Who you can switch to** | Subscriber, Customer | Roles staff can switch to. Admins are never included. Other options: Editor, Author, Contributor, Shop manager. |
| **After switching, open** | My account | Or **Shop**, **Cart** or **Home page**. |
| **Show a switch link on orders** | On | Adds **Switch to customer** to the order page. |

## Audit log

Every switch is logged under **WooCommerce → Status → Logs**, source `admin-powerups-switch-user`.

## Data

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | This power-up's saved settings. |

Records of switches that are still active expire on their own within 12 hours, even after the plugin is deleted.
