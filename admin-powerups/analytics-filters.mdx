Analytics order filters adds more choices to **Advanced filters** on WooCommerce's Orders report: where the order was sent or billed, how it was paid and shipped, and who ordered it.

**Shows in:** Analytics → Orders, under Advanced filters

<Note>
  This power-up needs WooCommerce 9.3 or newer and WooCommerce Analytics. Turn Analytics on in **WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → Features**. Until then the power-up won't switch on.
</Note>

## Turn it on

1. Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups** and switch on **Analytics order filters**.
2. Open **Analytics → Orders**, choose **Advanced filters**, and click **Add a filter**.

The summary numbers, chart, table and CSV export all follow the filters.

## The filters

| Filter | Match | How you pick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Billing country** or **Shipping country** | Includes / Excludes | Search and pick countries |
| **Billing state** or **Shipping state** | Includes / Excludes | Search and pick states |
| **Billing city** or **Shipping city** | Includes / Excludes | Type the city |
| **Payment method** | Is / Is not | Pick from your payment methods |
| **Shipping method** | Is / Is not | Pick a method type ("Any Flat rate") or a method in one zone |
| **Customer role** | Is / Is not | Pick a role, or **Guest (no account)** |

- **City** is an exact match. Your database decides whether case and accents matter. Commas are removed, so a city name that contains a comma can't match.
- **Payment method** and **Shipping method** list your current methods. Methods you've since removed can still be filtered through a link in the address bar.
- **Customer role** uses the customer's current role. **Guest (no account)** covers orders without an account. The list shows staff who can see reports which roles your paying customers have, for example admins or shop managers who ordered.
- Refunds follow their order's filters.

### Match all or any

With **Match any**, these filters combine with each other as "any", but WooCommerce's own filters (status, product and so on) still all apply.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Filters** | All on | Which filters appear: **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Payment method**, **Shipping method**, **Customer role**. Shipping method and Customer role are marked **Slower**. |
| **Address to match** | Billing address | Used by the country, state and city filters. Orders without a shipping address never match a shipping location, and always pass an "Excludes" filter. There's no fallback to the billing address. |

## Safe by design

If the filters can't be applied or counted, the report shows an error instead of unfiltered numbers:

> The Analytics order filters couldn't be applied, so no results are shown. Remove them or try again.

An invalid value, or too many values, shows no orders rather than all orders.

## Performance

<Warning>
  Filtered reports skip WooCommerce's report cache, so each one is counted fresh. **Shipping method** and **Customer role** look inside order items or customer accounts: on a store with 100,000 orders, a year-long report can take 1 to 2 seconds.
</Warning>

See [Performance](/admin-powerups/performance).

## Known limitations

- Up to 50 values per filter (20 for cities).
- **Segment by** isn't available while these filters are in use.
- An export started before the power-up (or a filter) was switched off sends its remaining parts unfiltered.
- For developers: inside a filtered Orders report, another Orders report query run by other code (without filters of its own) uses the same filters.
