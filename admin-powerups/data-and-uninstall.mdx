Every power-up lists what it stores in a **Data** section, so you can see it and delete it. Nothing is hidden, and nothing leaves your site.

## Turning a power-up off

Turning a power-up off keeps its settings and data. Switch it back on and everything is as you left it.

Turning it off does stop its background work. For example, it cancels guest-account conversions and order completions that are still waiting.

## The Data section

Open a power-up with **Configure** and scroll below its settings to **Data**. It lists what the power-up stores, how much, and a button for each item:

- **Delete** removes that item.
- **Reset** on **Settings** brings back the default settings.
- Items marked **Stays** belong to your store and are never deleted.

{/* Screenshot: the Data section of a power-up with Delete, Reset and Stays rows */}

Deleting asks you to confirm in two steps. Deleting all customer tags also asks you to type "delete".

Big lists, such as background job history, delete in batches with a progress count. When it's done, the page reloads so the settings show the new state, and a message confirms it.

### What stays

These are part of your store, so they're shown as **Stays** and never deleted, not even when you delete the plugin:

- Customer accounts created or linked from guest orders
- Addresses saved to customer profiles
- Completed orders and order notes

### What each power-up stores

Each power-up's page in these docs has a **Data** table. In short:

| Power-up | Stores |
|---|---|
| Every power-up with settings | Its settings |
| Accounts for guest orders | Welcome email markers, background job history |
| Advanced order search | Checked metadata keys, background job history |
| Autocomplete orders | Email markers, background job history |
| Customer table columns | Found columns |
| Customer tags | Tag groups and tags, background job history |
| Duplicate customer finder | Last scan results, scan in progress, "Not a duplicate" marks, scan work tables |
| Email log | Logged emails |
| Checkout blocklist | Blocked attempts, background job history |
| High-value order alerts | Email wording, alert sent markers, alert locks |

## Deleting the plugin

When you delete the plugin from **Plugins**, it removes its settings and data on every site.

To keep them, switch on **Keep data if the plugin is deleted** in the sidebar's System card before you delete the plugin. It's set per site.

Store records (accounts, saved addresses, orders and order notes) stay either way. Records of **Switch to customer** switches that are still active expire on their own within 12 hours.

## Privacy

- The plugin has no tracking. Your data stays on your site.
- [Customer tags](/admin-powerups/customer-tags) and the [Email log](/admin-powerups/email-log) are included in WordPress's personal data export and erase tools.
- [Accounts for guest orders](/admin-powerups/guest-accounts) creates user accounts from checkout data. Mention it in your privacy policy.
