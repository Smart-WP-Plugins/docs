Payment method rules hides payment methods, or only offers them, when the cart matches your conditions. For example: no Cash on delivery over $1,000, or bank transfer only for wholesale customers.

**Shows in:** Checkout

## Turn it on

1. Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups** and click **Configure** on **Payment method rules**.
2. Add your rules, click **Save changes**, and switch the power-up on.

With no rules, every method is offered as set up in WooCommerce.

## Build a rule

Click **Add rule**. Each rule reads like a sentence:

> **When** [all / any] **of these are true** … **Then** [hide / only offer] [payment methods]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick all or any">
    Choose whether **all** conditions or **any** of them must be true.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions">
    Click **Add condition**, then pick a condition, a comparison and a value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the action and methods">
    Choose **hide** or **only offer**, and pick the payment methods.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Conditions

| Condition | Comparisons | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cart total** | is more than, is at least, is less than, is at most | Items after discounts, with tax, without shipping. |
| **Items in cart** | is more than, is at least, is less than, is at most | |
| **Product category** | is any of, is none of | Includes subcategories. |
| **Product type** | is any of, is none of | Includes **Virtual** and **Downloadable**. |
| **Shipping class** | is any of, is none of | |
| **Chosen shipping method** | is any of, is none of | |
| **Country** | is any of, is none of | |
| **State** | is any of, is none of | |
| **Customer role** | is any of, is none of | Includes **Guest (not logged in)**. |

### Hide or only offer

- **Hide** removes the methods when the conditions match.
- **Only offer** shows the methods only when the conditions match.

Rules only ever remove methods, so their order doesn't matter.

## Examples

- **No Cash on delivery over $1,000**: When all of these are true: Cart total is more than 1000. Then hide Cash on delivery.
- **Bank transfer only for wholesale**: When all of these are true: Customer role is any of Wholesale. Then only offer Direct bank transfer.

## Where the rules apply

- The classic checkout and the block checkout.
- The "Pay for order" page (classic or block). There the rules check that order's total and items, not the cart.
- Admin screens always see every method.

## Saving

- A rule without methods, or with a condition missing its value, shows "Not saved until it has at least one method and a value in every condition." and isn't saved.
- If the rules can't be read when saving (a broken browser request), your saved rules are kept instead of being wiped.
