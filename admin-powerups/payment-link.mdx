Payment link helper gives you an unpaid order's payment link on the order page, ready to copy, open or email. It's handy for phone and manual orders.

**Shows in:** Order page, Customer pages box

## Turn it on

Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups** and switch on **Payment link helper**. Open any order: the **Payment link** section appears in the **Customer pages** box in the sidebar.

<Note>
The **Customer pages** box holds both the payment link and the [thank-you page preview](/admin-powerups/thankyou-preview). It only shows when one of them is on.
</Note>

{/* Screenshot: the Customer pages box with the Payment link section */}

## Use it

For unpaid orders you get three buttons:

- **Copy** copies the link. The button turns into a tick.
- **Open** opens the payment page in a new tab.
- **Email** asks you to confirm ("Send the payment link to …?"), then sends WooCommerce's "Order details" email, which includes the link. The order needs a billing email.

If the order belongs to a customer account, the customer is asked to log in to pay. That's a WooCommerce rule.

Paid orders show "Nothing to pay".

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Add an order note when the link is emailed** | On | Records who sent it and when. |

## Data

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | This power-up's saved settings. |
| **Order notes about sent links** | Stays. Order notes are part of your order history. |
