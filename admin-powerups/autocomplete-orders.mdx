Autocomplete orders moves orders straight to **Completed** when your rules match, for example paid orders for virtual products. Paid orders that match skip Processing.

WooCommerce on its own only completes orders where every item is both virtual and downloadable. This power-up lets you choose the rules.

**Shows in:** Order status after payment

## Turn it on

1. Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups**, click **Configure** on **Autocomplete orders**, and tick at least one rule under **Complete an order when**.
2. Click **Save changes**, then switch the power-up on.

<Note>
With no rules ticked, nothing changes, not even the exceptions.
</Note>

## Rules

Under **Complete an order when**, tick any of:

- **The order is paid**
- **Every item is virtual**
- **Every item is downloadable**
- **It has selected products**
- **It has products in selected categories** (subcategories count too)
- **It has products with selected tags**
- **It has products in selected shipping classes**
- **It was paid with a selected payment method**

**Rules needed** decides whether **Any checked rule** or **All checked rules** must match. The default is **Any checked rule**.

<Warning>
"The order is paid" completes every paid order, including ones you still need to ship. Every rule except the payment method rule also needs the order to be paid.
</Warning>

### Payment methods marked "Not paid yet"

Methods like **Direct bank transfer**, **Check payments** and **Cash on delivery** are marked **Not paid yet**. If you pick them, orders complete before the money arrives, and completed orders unlock downloads. Only pick them if you trust those customers.

"The order is paid" never counts cash on delivery orders, which reach Processing before any money arrives. Confirmed bank transfers and cheques count.

## Never complete when

These always win over the rules:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **The order has these products** | None | Skip orders with these products. |
| **The order has products in these categories** | None | Skip orders with products in these categories. |
| **The total is above** | 0 (no limit) | Leave big orders for a manual check. |
| **The customer left a note** | On | Someone should read it first. |
| **Also complete orders staff set to Processing** | Off | Off: changing a status by hand is left alone. |

The exceptions also stop WooCommerce's own completion of virtual and downloadable orders.

## Timing and email

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Wait before completing (minutes)** | 0 | 0 completes right away. A wait gives fraud checks and other plugins time to act. |
| **Send the "Order completed" email** | On | Turn off to complete orders without emailing the customer. |

A wait runs as a background job. If background jobs don't run on time on your site, you'll see a warning; see [Background jobs](/admin-powerups/getting-started#background-jobs). Switching the power-up off cancels completions that are still waiting.

Completing without the email doesn't stop you resending it by hand later. A completion that fails half way never silences a later "Completed order" email.

## What gets recorded

- Each completed order gets an order note that names the rule, starting "Completed automatically by Admin Powerups".
- Details are logged under **WooCommerce → Status → Logs**, source `admin-powerups-autocomplete`.

## When the rules run

The rules act when a payment completes. When staff move an order to Processing by hand, WooCommerce records the paid date as usual and the order isn't completed, unless **Also complete orders staff set to Processing** is on. Completing such an order by hand still sends the Completed email.

## Check and apply

The **Check and apply** tab needs the power-up to be on.

- **Check an order**: type an order number and click **Check** to see why it would or would not complete.
- **Orders waiting in Processing**: click **Count matching orders**, then **Complete matching orders** to apply your rules to orders already waiting. Emails follow your settings.

With custom order numbers, type the order number, not the internal ID.

## Data

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | This power-up's saved settings. |
| **Email markers** | Short-lived notes on orders that stop the "Completed order" email when you turned it off. |
| **Background job history** | Finished and failed background jobs. |
| **Completed orders and their notes** | Stays. Order statuses and notes are part of your order history. |

## Known limitations

- "Every item is virtual" and "Every item is downloadable" fail if a product on the order was deleted.
