Email recipients & attachments copies any WooCommerce email to extra addresses, such as your warehouse, accountant or supplier, and attaches files like terms, warranties or product guides.

**Shows in:** WooCommerce emails

## Turn it on

1. Go to **WooCommerce → Powerups**, click **Configure** on **Email recipients & attachments**.
2. Set up recipients and attachments, click **Save changes**, and switch the power-up on.

## Extra recipients

The **Extra recipients** table lists every WooCommerce email, split into **Store emails** and **Customer emails**. For each one you can add **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc** addresses. Separate addresses with commas. Emails turned off in WooCommerce are marked **Off in WooCommerce**.

On a phone, the table shows each email as a block: its name, then To, Cc and Bcc each on a labelled line.

{/* Screenshot: the Extra recipients table with a Bcc address on New order */}

<Warning>
Everyone in To and Cc sees each other's address, including the customer on customer emails. Use Bcc to keep addresses private.
</Warning>

- Invalid addresses are marked and not saved.
- Emails that are turned off, or have no recipient, stay unsent. An extra To address doesn't force them out.
- Account emails (new account, password reset) aren't offered, because password links go only to the account holder.

## Attachments

Click **Add attachment rule** to attach files to emails. Each rule has:

- **Files**: click **Choose files** to pick files from the Media Library.
- **Attach to**: the emails the files go with.
- **Only when the order has these products**, **…or products in these categories**: optional. Leave both empty to always attach.

<Warning>
Large files make emails slow to send and may be rejected by inboxes. Keep attachments small (under 5 MB in total).
</Warning>

Any Media Library file can be attached, including WooCommerce's protected downloadable files. Only store managers can set this up, but check what you attach.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Extra recipients** | None | Extra To, Cc and Bcc per email. |
| **Attachments** | None | Attachment rules. |

If the settings can't be read when saving (a broken browser request), your saved recipients and attachments are kept.

## Data

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | This power-up's saved settings, including recipients and attachment rules. The attached files stay in your Media Library. |
